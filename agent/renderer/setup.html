Zogency Agent

Connect this device to your agency's Zogency workspace.

This app reports your active vs idle time, the app in focus, its window title and (where available) the browser URL, and takes a screenshot of your screen every few minutes while you are active, sending them to your employer's Zogency server for productivity and company-data-protection review. This is a company-provided device for work use. Screenshots are retained for 14 days. It does not capture keystrokes, your clipboard, or passwords. You can pause or quit it any time from the tray icon; nothing is captured while paused or idle.
